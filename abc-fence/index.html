    Fence questions

    Straight answers. Anything else, call or text us.

    How much does a fence cost in Knoxville?

    It depends on the fence, the footage and the ground. Pick chain link or 6 ft pressure treated pine privacy in our price tool for a ballpark in about a minute. For everything else we come out and give you a free estimate.

    Are estimates free?

    Yes. We come to your property, measure the fence line and walk through options with you at no charge.

    What areas do you serve?

    Knoxville and the towns around it across East Tennessee, including Farragut, Powell, Kodak, Sevierville, Seymour, Maryville, Lenoir City, Oak Ridge, Clinton and Kingston. Not sure if you’re in range? Call and ask.

    Do you install automatic gate openers?

    Yes. We install solar and electric gate operators on roll gates and swing gates, along with keypads, exit probes and remotes.

    Can you build a custom gate?

    Yes. We weld and fabricate on site, so the gate is built to fit the opening you have, for homes, farms and businesses.

    Do you remove old fence and do repairs?

    Yes. We tear out old fence before the new one goes in, and we repair fences, gates and gate openers.

    What kind of wood do you use?

    Our wood privacy fences are pressure treated pine. Split rail is pine too, or cedar if you want it.

    Serving Knoxville and East TN

    We build fences in Knoxville, Farragut, Powell, Kodak, Sevierville, Seymour, Maryville, Lenoir City, Oak Ridge, Clinton and Kingston, and the towns around them. Not sure if you’re in range? Call and ask.
