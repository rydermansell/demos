Privacy

Updated November 15, 2026

Short version: the business you’re getting a quote from sees what you type into their quote tool so they can follow up about your job. Nobody else gets it. It’s never sold or used for ads. Want it deleted? Use the “Delete my info” link, reply STOP, or ask the business.

Who we are

PriceWink builds websites with quote and booking tools for local service businesses (for example, a fence or tree company). When you use one of those tools, the business is the one you’re dealing with, and PriceWink runs the tool for them. This page explains what happens to the information you enter.

What’s collected

  • What you type: your name, phone number, and anything else you choose to give, such as an address, email, notes, photos, the service you picked and the price you were shown.
  • Bookings: the time you booked and anything you add to it.
  • Unfinished quotes: if you type your name and phone number into the quote tool and leave before booking, what you entered is saved, as the notice next to those fields says. That lets the business follow up about your quote and lets you pick up where you left off.
  • Visit counts: the tool counts how many people looked at it, started a quote and finished one, and where visits came from (for example, a Facebook link). These counts have no names, numbers, cookies or device IDs attached. If you never type your contact info, you are only ever a number in a count.

How it’s used

  • To show you a price and book your visit.
  • To let the business contact you about your quote or booking by call, text or email, including one follow-up if you didn’t finish.
  • To send you emails you asked for, like a booking confirmation, a reminder, or a link back to your saved price.
  • To show the business simple totals, like how many people booked and where they came from.

Your information is never sold, never shared with advertisers, and never used to contact you about anything other than the job you asked about.

Who can see it

Only the business you requested a quote from, through their private, sign-in-protected inbox, and PriceWink when needed to run and support the tool. It’s stored with Google (Google Sheets, Calendar and Drive) under PriceWink’s account. Each business can only ever see its own customers.

How long it’s kept

  • Unfinished quotes are deleted after 30 days without activity.
  • Once you book, the details stay with your booking so the business can do the job, and the unfinished-quote copy is cleared within 7 days.
  • Links back to a saved price stop working after 7 days.
  • Visit counts are kept for about a month.

Your choices

  • Don’t want to be contacted? Reply STOP to a text, tell the business, or use the “Delete my info” link in any email or saved-quote page. The business can mark you as “don’t contact” and you won’t get follow-ups.
  • Want your info deleted or a copy of it? Ask the business, or email rj@getpricewink.com and tell us which business you used.
  • You never need to give your contact info to see a price.

Security

Business inboxes require each owner to sign in, and every request is checked on the server so one business can’t see another’s customers. Photos you upload are private to the business.

Children

These tools are for adults arranging work on their property. They aren’t meant for children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions: rj@getpricewink.com.
